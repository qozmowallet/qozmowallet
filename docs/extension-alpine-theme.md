# Qozmo Alpine artwork

The extension uses opaque surfaces and a locally bundled scenic photograph-style background. Since version 0.6.1, its warm white, charcoal and lavender interface matches the Qozmo website. Paper and ink inherit the shared website variables; status colors remain distinct. The photograph is decorative and contains no account data or official protocol branding. Existing Qozmo and Ethereum marks remain separate assets.

- Final asset: `public/assets/qozmo-alpine-stag.webp` (1280 pixels wide).
- Generated using the built-in imagegen tool on 11 November 2026. Converted to WebP with Sharp for packaging; no remote image request at runtime.
- Used on the Quantum extension balance card, welcome screen and locked screen.

Exact generation prompt:

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: scenic background for a professional Qozmo Quantum wallet extension, not a UI mockup.
Primary request: a beautiful alpine mountain landscape with a majestic deer stag.
Scene: layered mountain peaks, a quiet alpine lake and evergreen forest, soft morning mist.
Subject: one anatomically natural stag with antlers, standing on a rocky meadow in the right third of the composition, clearly visible.
Composition: wide landscape, mountains across the upper half, dark tranquil forest and lake on the left with clear negative space for white balance text. Stag fully within the right half with breathing room, visible at small wallet banner sizes. Landscape photograph, refined editorial quality.
Lighting: subtle warm dawn light, deep forest green and slate blue mountains, natural muted colors.
Constraints: background image only. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI, no frames, no cryptocurrency symbols, no glass effects, no watermark.
```
