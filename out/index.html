A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Simple answers.

A few things you might
be wondering about Qozmo.

Qozmo brings live markets and an EVM wallet workspace together. Create one encrypted Qozmo smart account on Robinhood Testnet for public ETH and ERC-20 transfers, Quantum authorization and Privacy Ring. The website shares the same address and unlock session across these features. Privacy notes require an additional encrypted backup; account imports remain read-only.

The hero streams public USD market quotes from Kraken for crypto, PAX Gold, stablecoins, and tokenized stocks and ETFs including Apple, NVIDIA, Microsoft, Tesla, S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100. The percentage is the provider’s rolling 24-hour change. Each timestamp shows the latest reported trade; less frequently traded assets can have older quotes even while the feed is connected. If the feed disconnects, the page labels the last received prices and retries automatically.

Select “Open wallet” to explore the dashboard and public market data. Create or import a wallet when you are ready to view your own balances. The Cards panel offers a separate 3D concept preview marked Coming soon.

The landing page shows illustrative spending and activity. Card designs are concepts marked Coming soon and cannot be ordered or used for payments. The crypto and RWA prices in the hero are live public market quotes. The dashboard reads your wallet balances; signing is available only for supported testnet transfers.

Download the extension ZIP, extract it, then open chrome://extensions in Chrome. Enable Developer mode, select Load unpacked, and choose the extracted qozmo-wallet-extension folder. The extension has its own local vault; website data is separate. The Extension section links to the full installation guide.

The Qozmo experience is currently a web preview, designed to work beautifully on mobile and desktop. An iOS or Android app is not available through this page yet.

The interactive preview is free to explore. Product pricing, supported countries, and payment services will be announced if and when the full wallet launches.
